Voice-First Expense Tracking with Zero Cloud Overhead

Kharchaji is a 100% offline-first personal bookkeeping app that lets you log expenses instantly using voice recognition and syncs backups exclusively to your private Google Drive.

🎙️ Voice Input 🔒 100% Offline-First ☁️ Google Drive AppData Sync 🚫 Zero Central Database

🎙️ Instant Voice Recognition

Log complex expenses with amount, category, and remarks in natural spoken Hindi or English directly on your device.

🔒 Total Data Sovereignty

Your ledger stays on your phone. No logins required, no tracking ads, and zero server storage of your financial numbers.

☁️ Private Google Drive Backup

Connect your personal Google Account to automatically sync compressed encrypted snapshots into your hidden Google Drive app folder.

🛡️ Our Privacy Guarantee

Kharchaji operates on a Zero-Server architecture. We do not operate any backend servers, databases, or user identity directories.

When you enable Google Drive Backup, Kharchaji uses the restricted drive.appdata scope to store your financial ledger strictly inside your own Google Drive's hidden application folder. Our app cannot read, modify, or access any other files in your Google Drive.
