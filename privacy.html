Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kharchaji ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Abhishek Soni, handles your information. We are committed to complete transparency, user privacy, and zero-server data sovereignty.

1. Zero-Server Architecture & Core Principles

Kharchaji is fundamentally an offline-first application. We do not host, operate, or maintain any backend servers or central databases. All your financial transactions, categories, budgets, and receipt photo attachments are stored locally on your device in private application storage.

2. Google User Data & Google Drive API Policy

Kharchaji offers an optional cloud backup and restore feature powered by the Google Drive REST API. When you connect your Google Account to Kharchaji:

  • Requested Scope: We strictly request the restricted scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (Application Data Folder).
  • Access Limitations: This scope grants Kharchaji access strictly to a hidden, app-specific folder inside your own Google Drive. Kharchaji cannot see, read, modify, or delete any of your personal files, Google Docs, photos, or standard Google Drive documents.
  • How We Use Google Data: The Google Drive API is used solely to upload and download your compressed, encrypted ledger backup archive (kharchaji_cloud_backup.zip) to keep your devices in sync and prevent accidental data loss.
  • No Transfer to Third Parties: Your Google user data, email address, credentials, and backup files are never transferred to, stored on, or processed by any external or third-party servers. All interactions occur directly between your device and Google's official servers.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
Kharchaji's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Audio Recording (Microphone): Used exclusively during active voice expense logging sessions on your device to parse amounts and categories. Voice recordings are never stored permanently or uploaded to any remote server.
  • Camera & Storage / Media: Used solely to allow you to take or attach receipt photos to your expense entries. These images remain stored locally on your device and inside your private Google Drive backup.
  • Notifications: Used on Android 13+ devices to alert you if your automatic background cloud sync encounters an authorization issue.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

Because Kharchaji does not store your information on external servers, you maintain 100% control over your data at all times:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can delete all local data at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings or using the "Clear All Data" button in the app's Settings screen.
  • Cloud Backup Deletion: You can delete your cloud backup directly inside Kharchaji by tapping Settings > Backup & Restore > Delete Cloud Backup. Alternatively, you can manage and delete hidden app data from the Google Drive web console under Settings > Manage Apps > Kharchaji > Options > Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke Kharchaji's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Third-Party Permissions page.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
